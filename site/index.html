What it does

From a window to a council meeting.

The whole job in one app: plan the view, aim the camera, calibrate it, leave it running, and walk into the meeting with numbers nobody can wave away.

Live tracking

Every car, boxed and timed.

Each vehicle gets a box and a speed as it crosses the second white line. Speeders light up amber, way-over red, wrong-way violet. It keeps running unattended, reconnects a camera that drops out, and warns you if the camera gets knocked out of line.

One-way streets

Wrong-way drivers, called out.

Tell VST which way is legal and every car going against it is flagged, counted and clipped, so you can show it, not just say it.

3wrong way today
Narrow views

Even a tiny front window.

A long view of a two-way street, or about 14 ft of a narrow one-way street seen at an angle. The site planner tells you what your window can time before you mount anything.

Numbers that hold up

Calibrate, then prove it.

Drive past at a known speed, use cones or a pole and a tape measure, or size it from a parked car. Then check it with a second drive. Every method adds to the others.

Private by design

Speeds, not people.

VST keeps each vehicle's speed, direction, time and a short clip for you to review. It never reads plates or recognizes faces, and nothing leaves your computer unless you share it.

Any camera to start

Use what you've got.

A webcam, an old phone in USB webcam mode, a network camera, a Raspberry Pi camera, or a recording. VST picks your camera's best mode, says in plain words what it can time, and only suggests an upgrade if you want one. No camera yet? Plan from a photo of your view.

For the meeting

A report a council can read.

The 85th-percentile speed traffic engineers use, the share over the limit, speeds by hour, and a printable street report written for neighbors and city staff. Archive “before the new signs” and compare after. Publish a dataset with checksums anyone can re-check.

Quick start

Five steps. VST walks you through each one.

The same five numbered steps you'll see down the side of the app. You can go back to any of them, and nothing is lost. Want to look first? Open VST Studio and press Watch the sample measure: eight minutes of Locust Avenue, measured in front of you.

On your phone

Your street, in your pocket.

Turn on Companion app in VST Studio and scan the QR code. Your phone watches the camera, lists the latest speeders and wrong-way drivers with clips, and runs setup from the curb while you aim and calibrate. It talks only to your computer, over your own Wi-Fi.

Try it now Open the demo → Simulated traffic on a one-way street, with video from the original 2016 recording. Works on any phone or desktop browser. Calibration drives VST Ride-along → No cruise control under 25 mph? Record your drive's GPS speed on your phone and VST Studio reads your exact speed from it. Add it to your home screen; it works with no signal.
